A quick audition before the event

Choose the shot

  1. Large, complete objects at an ordinary store camera angle. One to five targets work better than a hundred tiny ones.
  2. Keep the camera steady while objects enter, leave, or separate, so the count change is obvious.
  3. Evaluate the actual center crop on the TV and phone. Portrait footage may need an intentional crop.
  4. Watch the whole loop and sample evenly spaced frames. Reject scenes with repeated misses, wrong classes, or boxes that jump.

Choose the story

  • Checkout: people recognition is already dependable; do not imply loss prevention or dwell inference from boxes alone.
  • Produce: a 15-second phone clip with one apple, banana and orange placed separately on a plain counter may be more reliable than any crowded stock video.
  • Parking: enable COCO car/truck/bus and show one Vehicles row. It demonstrates counts, not occupancy or traffic analytics.
  • Stockroom: show a partner in context. Shelf gaps and SKU recognition require a different capability.

Model check: centered 1.4:1 crop, up to 640×457 pixels, the current SSD Lite MobileNet V2 COCO model, threshold .60 for people and .48 for other spot-checked classes. The car, parking and apple spot checks use one frame each; the checkout and produce clips had eight sampled frames each. CPU checks validate recognition, not event-device GPU speed or temporal stability.
